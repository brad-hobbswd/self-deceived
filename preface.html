Preface

Writing about obedience requires a willingness to stand beneath the same Word being discussed. This subject is not distant or theoretical. Scripture speaks to the choices we make, the motives we carry, and the ways we respond when truth asks something of us.

James 1:22 warns believers not to become hearers only, deceiving themselves. That warning is sobering precisely because self-deception can feel like confidence. We may believe we are growing simply because we recognize the language of faith, yet the measure of our response is found in whether the Word continues to direct our lives.

These pages are offered as an invitation to personal examination rather than an accusation against others. The author is included in the responsibility addressed here. None of us outgrows the need to receive correction, seek God’s mercy, and practice what we have learned.

The chapters move through distinct aspects of this responsibility, from the danger of hearing without doing to the freedom and blessing found in a life that continues in the Word. My prayer is that the reading will encourage honesty, humility, and a renewed willingness to follow Scripture.

Bradley Hobbs

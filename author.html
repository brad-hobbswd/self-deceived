About the Author

Bradley Hobbs is a Christian author, educator, minister, musician, and web designer whose writing is rooted in Scripture, spiritual growth, faith, and the questions that shape everyday life. His work seeks to communicate biblical truth with clarity while making room for honest reflection, practical application, and compassion.

Through teaching and writing, Bradley is drawn to subjects that invite readers to examine beliefs, relationships, experiences, and spiritual lives through the lens of Scripture. His books address faith, growth, healing, relationships, and the challenges of living with conviction.

Bradley believes the Word of God is not simply meant to be heard or understood, but received and lived. SELF-DECEIVED reflects that conviction and invites readers to approach Scripture with humility, honesty, and willingness to respond.

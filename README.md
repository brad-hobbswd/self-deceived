# SELF-DECEIVED

**When Religious Knowledge Replaces Obedience**  
By Bradley Hobbs

This repository contains the official website and online reading structure for *SELF-DECEIVED*, a ten-chapter biblical teaching manuscript centered on James 1:22 and the warning against hearing the Word without doing the Word.

## Book focus
The book examines religious knowledge, self-deception, conviction, repentance, practical conduct, speech, private integrity, freedom, and obedience. Its central message: **The Word we hear must become the Word we obey.**

## Pages

- Chapters: `chap1.html` through `chap10.html`

## Site design
Dark literary design, warm gold accents, responsive navigation, and reading-focused layout. Shared styles are in `style.css`; mobile menu behavior is in `script.js`.

## Manuscript status
Front matter and closing pages have been integrated. Chapter pages are connected and prepared; the full previously drafted chapter text remains to be inserted so exact manuscript wording and continuity are preserved.

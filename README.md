# SELF-DECEIVED

## When Religious Knowledge Replaces Obedience
**By Bradley Hobbs**

*SELF-DECEIVED* is a ten-chapter biblical teaching manuscript examining the difference between hearing, knowing, and obeying the Word of God. Built around James 1:22, the book calls readers to allow Scripture to examine their lives and lead them toward practical obedience.

> **The Word we hear must become the Word we obey.**

## About the Book

Religious familiarity can create confidence without producing spiritual maturity. A person may know Scripture, speak about faith, attend church, serve, and still resist the truth in an area of life. This book explores how self-deception develops and how conviction, repentance, and obedience bring a person back into alignment with the Word.

The chapters address:

- Hearing the Word without doing it
- Knowledge becoming a substitute for obedience
- Self-justification and the danger of overlooking our own hearts
- Scripture as the standard and mirror
- Conviction, repentance, and changed behavior
- Obedience in ordinary decisions and responsibilities
- Speech as a reflection of the heart
- Integrity when no one is watching
- Freedom, forgiveness, and wise boundaries
- Continuing faithfully in the Word

The writing uses biblical teaching alongside familiar everyday situations, inviting personal examination rather than judgment of others.

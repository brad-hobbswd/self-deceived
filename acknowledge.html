Acknowledgements

First, I give thanks to God for His Word, His correction, His mercy, and His patience. Scripture has remained the foundation of this work, and the reminder that truth calls for obedience has challenged the writer as deeply as the reader.

This book is not a claim of perfect obedience. Writing these pages has also required personal examination, humility, and willingness to recognize the places where the Word must continue to shape my own life.

I am grateful for the people whose lives, conversations, experiences, questions, and circumstances have contributed to reflection and understanding. I appreciate my family and those close to me for their presence, patience, encouragement, and love.

To every reader who takes time to examine their life honestly before God, thank you. My hope is that these pages encourage a response to Scripture that is sincere, practical, and ongoing.

Above all, I acknowledge the enduring authority of the Word of God, which reveals truth, corrects our direction, and calls us to live what we have heard.

Bradley Hobbs

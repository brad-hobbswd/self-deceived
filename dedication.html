Dedication

This book is dedicated to those who have heard the Word, recognized the truth, and wrestled with the responsibility of living according to what they have received. May every person who encounters these pages find the courage to face the mirror of Scripture and choose correction over justification, repentance over excuses, and truth over appearances.

Above all, this work is dedicated to Jesus Christ, the Word, the truth that exposes deception, the mercy that restores, and the authority that calls us to obedience.

May we never become so familiar with the Word that we forget to obey the One who gave it.
SELF-DECEIVED · Bradley Hobbs
